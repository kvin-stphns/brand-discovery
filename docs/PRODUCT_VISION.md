# Product Vision

## Platform

Discovery Studios is a fashion discovery platform for luxury retail, high-end boutiques, independent designers, and culturally relevant product curation. The experience should feel editorial, precise, and premium without becoming a static magazine or a generic marketplace grid.

## Core Experiences

- Home: a polished entry point into featured products, popular momentum, and rankings.
- Featured: curated product/brand/designer highlights using normalized product data.
- Popular: DB-backed trending products and retailers based on click/vote/recency signals.
- Explore/Discover: category-aware discovery across women, men, gifts, and general explore.
- Product detail: complete product story with brand, retailer, price, images, description, details, sizes, availability, shipping/returns, cart, and checkout handoff.
- Cart: grouped by retailer/source so multi-retailer checkout is honest and clear.
- Affiliate checkout: smart redirect to affiliate URL when present, otherwise canonical URL with UTM parameters.
- Rankings/leaderboard: product-aware rankings that work in test mode and can grow into real popularity intelligence.
- Location: a future boutique/retailer location layer that should never present mock data as live data.
- Admin/status dashboard: data health, feed import status, product counts, source counts, and quality counts.

## Retailer Value

The MVP should help retailers and boutiques see:

- Their products can be presented with brand-safe, high-end visual polish.
- Catalog data can be normalized from feeds without scraping dependence.
- Affiliate clicks are logged and can be attributed.
- Products are not shown when core fields are missing or low quality.
- Discovery Studios understands feed/API partnership requirements and can respect image, inventory, and deeplink rules.

## Strategic Difference

Discovery Studios is not trying to scrape its way into production. Scraping can help prototype coverage, test enrichment, or recover missing metadata, but approved product catalogs are the foundation for scale, accuracy, and retailer trust.
