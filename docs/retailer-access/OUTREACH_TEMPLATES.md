## 5. “Why Discovery Studios” Pitch Paragraph

Discovery Studios is a curated discovery layer for high-end fashion, luxury boutiques, and culturally relevant retail. It presents products in a polished editorial interface with DB-backed catalog data, rankings, product detail pages, cart grouping by retailer, and affiliate checkout handoff. The platform is being built feed-first, so approved retailers can provide official catalog data instead of relying on fragile scraping. For partners, the value is brand-safe product presentation, qualified referral traffic, attribution-ready outbound clicks, and a scalable way to surface boutique inventory to shoppers who are actively looking to discover distinctive products.

## 6. Technical Data Request Checklist

- Product feed/API access.
- Feed format: CSV, JSON, XML, API, SFTP, affiliate network feed, or private export.
- Stable product ID/SKU.
- Brand/designer name.
- Product title.
- Canonical product URL.
- Affiliate/deeplink URL support.
- Product image URLs.
- Image usage permissions and restrictions.
- Price, currency, original price, sale price.
- Inventory/availability.
- Sizes/variants.
- Color/material/composition.
- Category/breadcrumb path.
- Description and detail bullets.
- Shipping and returns summary.
- Update frequency.
- Regional/currency coverage.
- Deeplink tracking rules.
- Attribution window.
- Prohibited keywords or placement rules.
- Contact for feed troubleshooting.
